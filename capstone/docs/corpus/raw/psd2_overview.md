# PSD2 overview

## Public summary

The revised Payment Services Directive, PSD2, is European Union legislation on payment services. It became
applicable in January 2018. The directive aims to make internet payment services easier and safer, to better
protect consumers against fraud, abuse and payment problems, to promote innovative mobile and internet
payment services, and to strengthen consumer rights.

## Strong customer authentication

The strong customer authentication (SCA) requirement came into force on 13 September 2019. The European
Commission page gives the date but not the detailed rules or exemptions, so those must be checked in the
technical standards.

## Open banking and new providers

PSD2 introduced a new category of payment service provider beyond banks, which increases competition and
choice for consumers. It also strengthened the role of the European Banking Authority (EBA), which
coordinates supervisory authorities and drafts technical standards.

## Consumer protection and card fees

The directive strengthens consumer rights and is part of a legislative package that limits fees for consumer
debit and credit card transactions. It bans retailer surcharges for card use.

## Revision

PSD2 is being revised. Legislative proposals for PSD3 and PSR were published on 28 June 2023, and a political
agreement on the review was reached on 27 November 2025, the date shown on the Commission page.
