# RBI authentication directions, 2025

## Public summary

The Reserve Bank of India (Authentication mechanisms for digital payment transactions) Directions, 2025 were
issued on 25 September 2025. They apply to payment system providers and payment system participants, which include
banks and non-bank entities, for domestic digital payment transactions. The directions require compliance by 1 April
2026 unless a provision says otherwise.

## Two factors of authentication

All digital payment transactions must be authenticated by at least two distinct factors of authentication unless
they are exempted. A factor of authentication is a credential of the customer that is used for authentication. It
can be something the user has, something the user knows or something the user is, for example a password, an SMS
one-time password, a PIN, a card, a software token, a fingerprint or another form of biometrics.

## Dynamic factor

For digital payment transactions other than card-present transactions, at least one of the factors must be
dynamically created or proven, which means that the proof sent with the transaction is unique to that transaction.

## Exemptions

Annexure 1 lists seven exempt categories: small-value contactless card transactions, recurring e-mandate
transactions after the first, certain prepaid instruments, NETC transactions, small-value offline digital payments,
travel bookings made with corporate or commercial cards, and tap-and-pay UPI at NFC-enabled point-of-sale machines.

## Issuer responsibilities

Issuers must ensure the robustness and integrity of the authentication mechanism before deployment. They must
compensate the customer in full without demur for a loss that arises from non-compliance with the directions. They
must also comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## Cross-border card-not-present transactions

By 1 October 2026, card issuers must have mechanisms to validate non-recurring cross-border card-not-present
transactions, and they must apply risk-based handling to all such transactions.

## Earlier circulars repealed

Annexure 2 lists eight earlier circulars from 2009 to 2016 that the directions repeal. They include the circular
of 6 December 2016 that relaxed additional-factor authentication for card-not-present payments up to ₹2,000
(DPSS.CO.PD No. 1431/02.14.003/2016-17).
